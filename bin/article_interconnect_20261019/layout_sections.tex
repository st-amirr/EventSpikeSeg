Exact cutoffs and source hashes are retained in the vector manifest. Zooming preserves represented vector geometry. Native device and bank details are shown in the main manuscript. The GDS is unchanged. Rendering scripts were prepared with AI assistance. SVG and PDF exports are supplied for all 25 regions.
